Frequently Asked Questions: Web Design in Kano

Transparent advice on costs, timelines, hosting, and local search visibility.

How much does a professional business website cost in Kano?
The investment required depends entirely on your project specifications. A straightforward corporate website with 5 to 7 pages requires less engineering than a multi-vendor e-commerce store with automated Paystack checkout, inventory management, or a school portal with student result checking. Our pricing includes .com or .com.ng domain registration, high-speed cloud hosting, SSL certificates, custom mobile-friendly UI design, and technical on-page SEO. We provide itemized, honest proposals tailored to your budget.
How long does it take Amee Technology to complete and launch a website?
Standard business and corporate websites typically take between 2 and 4 weeks from project kickoff to public launch. More complex e-commerce stores or custom web applications requiring custom databases and multi-tier user logins generally take 4 to 6 weeks.
Can you register a Nigerian .com.ng or .ng domain name for our business?
Yes. We register all Nigerian top-level domains (.com.ng, .ng, .edu.ng, .org.ng) as well as global domains (.com, .org, .net). We also configure corporate email accounts matching your domain name (e.g., info@yourcompany.com.ng) to give your brand instant professional credibility.
Will our website rank on Google when customers in Kano search for our services?
Yes. We build every page with local technical SEO best practices: LocalBusiness schema markup specifying your Kano location, localized meta titles and descriptions, mobile speed optimization, and direct submission to Google Search Console. We also assist with Google Business Profile optimization to maximize your visibility in local Kano search results.
Do we need technical skills to update text, products, or blog articles?
No technical background is required. We structure your website with intuitive content management capabilities. We provide hands-on video tutorials, comprehensive documentation, and ongoing support retainers so you can easily update products, write new articles, or adjust pricing anytime.
Will the website display properly on low-end Android smartphones?
Absolutely. Over 85% of web visitors across Kano and Northern Nigeria browse on mobile devices. We design mobile-first and test every site across popular entry-level Android devices and high-end screens to guarantee seamless responsiveness and minimal data consumption.
Can customers pay online with Naira debit cards or bank transfers?
Yes. We integrate verified Nigerian payment gateways including Paystack, Monnify, and Flutterwave. Customers can pay using Mastercard, Visa, Verve, USSD, or automated bank transfer, with immediate email and WhatsApp notifications sent to your sales team upon payment completion.
Can we visit or meet with Amee Technology in Kano?
Our engineering office is located along Zoo Road, Kano, Kano State. We welcome scheduled in-person project meetings with local business executives and entrepreneurs, and we are also available for convenient Google Meet or WhatsApp video consultations.
