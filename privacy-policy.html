Privacy Policy

At Amee Technology Ltd (accessible from https://ameetechnology.com.ng), protecting the privacy and security of our visitors and clients is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we collect, how we process and store it, and your rights regarding your personal data.

If you have additional questions or require further details about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@ameetechnology.com.ng.

Consent

By using our website and services, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Information We Collect

The personal information requested and the reasons you are asked to provide it will be made clear at the point of request. This may include:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and organization name.
  • Project Details: Information, briefs, technical requirements, or messages sent when inquiring about custom software or consulting services.
  • Interactive Assessment Data: Responses provided through our AI assessment tool (AmeeBot) to generate customized diagnostic reports.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Deliver, operate, and maintain our digital products and websites.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our services.
  • Analyze website usage trends to enhance performance and user experience.
  • Communicate with you for customer support, project consultations, and promotional updates.
  • Detect and prevent technical issues, fraud, and security vulnerabilities.

Log Files

Amee Technology Ltd follows standard procedures of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Like any modern website, Amee Technology Ltd uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited, optimizing users' experience.

Data Protection Rights (GDPR & NDPR)

In compliance with Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and international privacy frameworks, every user is entitled to:

  • The right to access: Request copies of your personal data held by us.
  • The right to rectification: Request that we correct any inaccurate information.
  • The right to erasure: Request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing: Limit how we process your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our privacy desk at support@ameetechnology.com.ng.
