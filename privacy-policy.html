1. About Us & Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data collected and processed by Amee Technology Ltd (referred to as "Amee Technology", "Ameetech", "we", "us", or "our"), an incorporated technology and software development company based in Kano State, Nigeria.

This policy governs personal data handled through:

  • Our primary website at https://ameetechnology.com.ng;
  • Our online business productivity platform at https://toolkits.ameetechnology.com.ng;
  • Our interactive diagnostic assessment tool (AmeeBot AI);
  • Our educational materials, articles, and Business Academy downloads;
  • Direct client software engineering, mobile application, web development, UI/UX, and AI consulting engagements;
  • Communications with us via WhatsApp, email, contact forms, telephone, or in-person business meetings.

For the purposes of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Amee Technology Ltd acts as the Data Controller for personal data collected through these channels.

2. Our Data Protection Principles

In adherence to Section 24 of the NDPA 2023, Amee Technology commits to the following foundational data processing principles:

  • Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency: Personal data is processed on legitimate statutory grounds and communicated with complete openness.
  • Purpose Limitation: Data is collected only for specified, explicit, and legitimate commercial or technical purposes and not further processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes.
  • Data Minimization: We only collect information that is adequate, relevant, and strictly limited to what is necessary for our stated purposes.
  • Accuracy: We take every reasonable step to ensure personal data is accurate, complete, and kept up to date.
  • Storage Limitation: Personal data is kept in an identifiable form for no longer than is necessary for the purposes for which it is processed.
  • Integrity and Confidentiality: Data is secured using modern technical and organizational safeguards against unauthorized processing, accidental loss, damage, or destruction.
  • Accountability: We maintain documentation, logs, and verifiable controls demonstrating active compliance with the NDPA 2023.

3. Information We Collect

The categories of data we collect depend on how you interact with our website, services, and software solutions:

  • Contact and Identity Information: Full name, official email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, company/organization name, and physical office location when submitting project briefs, contact forms, or support inquiries.

No document information or financial records are uploaded to, stored on, or transmitted across Amee Technology servers.

8. International Data Transfers

As an entity operating in Nigeria, some of our cloud hosting and technical sub-processors operate servers internationally (such as in the European Union or United States).

In accordance with Part VI (Sections 41–43) of the NDPA 2023, when personal data is transferred outside Nigeria, we ensure that:

  • The recipient country offers an adequate level of data protection as recognized by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC); or
  • The transfer is governed by binding Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or Data Protection Agreements incorporating robust technical security and data subject rights enforcement; or
  • The data subject has provided explicit, informed consent for the specific transfer after being apprised of potential risks.

9. Data Security & Storage Safeguards

We enforce multi-layered defense-in-depth security measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Transport Encryption: All web traffic is strictly served over modern HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • Content Security Policy (CSP): We enforce a strict CSP forbidding unauthorized inline scripts, restricting frames (frame-ancestors 'self'), and eliminating cross-site scripting (XSS) vectors.
  • Input Sanitization: All user inputs across AmeeBot, search components, and forms undergo strict HTML entity escaping and server-side validation.
  • Access Controls & Authentication: Internal administrative dashboards enforce strong, randomly generated credentials, role-based access control, and constant audit logging.
  • Vulnerability Management: Automated regression security testing suites run continuously across all repository codebases.

11. Your Data Protection Rights

Under Part V (Sections 34–39) of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and international data privacy regulations (GDPR), you possess comprehensive rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Be Informed: To receive clear, transparent, and easily understandable information about how we collect and process your personal data.
  • Right of Access: To obtain confirmation of whether we process your data and request a readable copy of that personal data without charge.
  • Right to Rectification: To have inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete personal data corrected or updated.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): To require the permanent deletion or removal of your personal data where there is no compelling legal reason for its continued processing.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: To pause or limit the processing of your personal data under specific circumstances (e.g. while data accuracy is contested).
  • Right to Data Portability: To receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format for transfer to another controller.
  • Right to Object: To object at any time to the processing of your data based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing communications.
  • Rights Regarding Automated Decision-Making: You will not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing or profiling that produce legal or significantly adverse effects.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing conducted prior to withdrawal.

12. Information Required for Data Deletion (Right to Erasure)

You have the statutory right to request the permanent deletion of your personal records from our databases and marketing systems.

Verification Required to Prevent Fraud: To protect you from unauthorized, malicious, or fraudulent deletion requests by third parties, we must verify your identity before executing a deletion request.

Checklist of Information Required to Process a Deletion Request:

  1. Full Legal Name: The name associated with your inquiries, consulting contracts, or Academy downloads.
  2. Primary Registered Email Address: The exact email address submitted through our forms or correspondence. Your request must originate from or be confirmed via this email.
  3. Telephone / WhatsApp Number: If provided during earlier communications, to confirm matching identity records.
  4. Organization / Company Name: If your inquiries were made on behalf of an enterprise client.
  5. Specific Scope of Deletion: State whether you require:
    • Complete Erasure: Total removal of all contact logs, AmeeBot submissions, Academy lead lists, and marketing emails; or
    • Specific Erasure: Removal from email newsletters or specific project logs only.

How to Submit Your Request:

Submit your deletion request directly to our Data Protection Desk at support@ameetechnology.com.ng with the subject line Data Deletion Request - [Your Full Name].

Turnaround Time: We will acknowledge your request within 48 business hours and execute complete deletion across active systems within 30 calendar days, free of charge, in accordance with the NDPA 2023.

For complete instructions, statutory exceptions, and our interactive mailto request generator, visit our dedicated Data Deletion Policy & Instructions Page.

13. Children's Privacy

Our services, enterprise software platforms, and business tools are directed exclusively at business operators, professionals, and adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children under the age of 18 without verifiable parental or guardian consent.

If we discover that a minor under 18 has provided personal information to us without requisite legal authorization, we will promptly delete such records from our databases. If you suspect that a child has provided us with personal information, please notify us immediately at support@ameetechnology.com.ng.
