Data Deletion Policy & Request Instructions

Published by Amee Technology Ltd · Updated: October 30, 2026 · Kano State, Nigeria

Your Right to Permanent Data Erasure Under Section 34 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) and Article 17 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), every individual has the legal right to request that Amee Technology Ltd permanently erase their personal data from our active databases, mailing lists, and systems without undue delay.

1. Information Required to Delete Personal Data

To ensure that data deletion requests are authentic and to prevent malicious actors from attempting to delete legitimate customer or learner records, you must provide the following information when submitting a deletion request:

Required Information Why It Is Needed Mandatory / Optional
1. Full Legal Name Matches the identity records in our CRM, customer inquiries, and lead systems. Mandatory
2. Primary Email Address The exact email address you submitted when contacting us, downloading Academy guides, or using AmeeBot. Requests must originate from or be verified via this address. Mandatory
3. Phone / WhatsApp Number Helps confirm matching database records if you communicated via WhatsApp or phone consultation. Optional (if used)
4. Organization / Business Name Identifies corporate project briefs or enterprise consulting inquiries. Optional (if used)
5. Specific Scope of Deletion Specify the exact records you wish erased:
  • Complete Deletion: All email lists, consultation records, AmeeBot assessments, and contact forms.
  • Academy Unsubscribe & Lead Deletion: Removal from educational download emails only.
  • AmeeBot Diagnostic Data: Removal of self-reported business diagnostic answers.
 Mandatory

2. Identity Verification Protocol

To safeguard your privacy and comply with NDPA security standards, we enforce a strict verification protocol:

  • If you send your deletion request from the registered email address, we will reply with a confirmation token to verify access.
  • If a deletion request is received from an alternate or unverified address, we will require secondary confirmation from your primary registered channel before executing deletion.
  • We will never ask you for banking PINs, card CVVs, or account passwords during verification.

3. Step-by-Step Deletion Procedure

Step 1: Submit Your Request

Send an email to our Data Protection Desk at support@ameetechnology.com.ng with the subject line Data Deletion Request - [Your Full Name], containing the required checklist information.

Step 2: Acknowledgment & Identity Verification

Within 48 to 72 business hours, our privacy officer will acknowledge receipt and verify your identity via email confirmation.

Step 3: Database & System Deletion Execution

Upon successful verification, our technical engineering team executes permanent deletion or anonymization across our marketing platforms, CRM logs, and backups within 30 calendar days, completely free of charge.

Step 4: Formal Written Confirmation

Once deletion is complete, you will receive a final confirmation email confirming that your personal records have been permanently expunged.

5. Statutory Processing Timelines

  • Initial Acknowledgment: Within 48–72 business hours of receipt.
  • Execution of Deletion: Completed within 30 calendar days.
  • Fee: Exercising your right to erasure is completely free of charge.
  • Extension: In rare cases involving complex multi-platform enterprise accounts, the NDPA permits an extension of up to 30 additional days, with prior written notification explaining the reason for the delay.

6. Legal & Statutory Retention Exceptions

In accordance with Section 34(2) of the NDPA 2023 and GDPR Article 17(3), we cannot delete personal data where retention is mandated by prevailing statutory laws or legal necessity:

  • Tax & Commercial Financial Records: Nigerian commercial law (Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 - CAMA) and Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) statutes mandate that commercial invoices, VAT records, and payment confirmations must be preserved for 6 years.
  • Dispute Resolution & Legal Defense: Data required to defend against ongoing legal claims, contract disputes, or enforce signed contracts.
  • Cybersecurity & Fraud Prevention: Automated network security logs maintained for legitimate security analysis and defense against criminal cyber attacks.
