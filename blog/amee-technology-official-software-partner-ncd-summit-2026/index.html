AMEE TECHNOLOGY LTD is proud to announce its partnership with Northern Creative Designers Summit 2.0 (NCD Summit 2026), where the company has been officially named the Software Partner for the upcoming event.

Scheduled to convene in Kano on 19 December 2026, the Northern Creative Designers Summit represents the premier annual gathering uniting designers, software developers, and visual storytellers across Northern Nigeria. The official announcement establishes Ameetech as a key technical collaborator in advancing creative excellence and digital infrastructure across the region.

Advancing Creativity and Digital Innovation in Northern Nigeria

The partnership reflects a shared commitment to advancing creativity, technology, and digital innovation within Northern Nigeria's rapidly growing design ecosystem.

Across Kano, Kaduna, Abuja, and broader regional creative hubs, modern enterprises, independent design studios, and freelance professionals increasingly rely on robust digital tools to develop their ideas, deploy products, and deliver sustained commercial value. As design disciplines converge with software engineering, modern infrastructure continues to play an essential role in supporting the creative industry's structural expansion.

Through this partnership, AMEE TECHNOLOGY joins an active community working collaboratively to shape the next chapter of Arewa design, ensuring local creative talents are equipped to compete effectively on national and global stages.

The Intersection of Software Engineering and Creative Design

For Ameetech, the announcement represents an important opportunity to contribute to substantive conversations surrounding the intersection of software, user experience, and digital innovation, while strengthening meaningful connections within the region's creative community.

As an engineering firm delivering high-performance custom software, artificial intelligence workflows, and UI/UX design systems, Ameetech recognises that impactful software products require both rigorous engineering discipline and human-centred design aesthetics.

By participating as the official Software Partner, Ameetech reinforces its dedication to fostering collaborative spaces where technical builders and visual designers exchange expertise, elevate industry standards, and pioneer digital solutions tailored to regional market realities.

"As businesses, designers, and creative professionals increasingly rely on digital tools to develop their ideas and deliver value, technology continues to play an important role in supporting the creative industry's growth. Through this partnership, AMEE TECHNOLOGY joins a community working to shape the next chapter of Arewa design."

Building a Stronger, More Connected Creative Ecosystem

AMEE TECHNOLOGY looks forward to supporting initiatives that bring together creativity, technology, and innovation across Northern Nigeria. As preparations for NCD Summit 2.0 continue, this partnership highlights the enduring value of cross-disciplinary collaboration in building a stronger and more resilient digital economy.

By aligning technical capability with creative ambition, Ameetech remains dedicated to developing local tech capacity, empowering Nigerian founders, and demonstrating that enterprise-grade software and design excellence thrive right here in Northern Nigeria.
