FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our custom software development process.

Who owns the source code and Intellectual Property (IP)?

You do. 100%. Upon completion and final settlement, you receive the complete Git repository, database scripts, server credentials, and full legal ownership of the codebase and intellectual property.

How long does a typical custom software project take?

A focused MVP or business platform typically ships within 3 to 6 weeks. Larger enterprise systems or multi-sided marketplaces generally take 8 to 12 weeks with weekly demo milestones throughout.

Can you integrate local Nigerian payment gateways and VTU APIs?

Yes, we have deep hands-on expertise integrating Paystack, Flutterwave, Monnify, Squad, Remita, and telecommunications VTU APIs for automated airtime, data vending, and electricity tokens.

Do you offer maintenance and support after launch?

Yes. Every custom software project includes 30 days of comprehensive warranty and bug-fixing post-launch, alongside flexible ongoing retainer plans for server maintenance, security patches, and feature additions.
