FAQ

Growth Marketing Questions Answered

Answers to common questions about marketing timelines and budgets.

How long before we see results from SEO?

Technical indexing and initial keyword ranking improvements typically appear within 30 to 45 days. Substantial, predictable organic inbound lead volume typically solidifies within 60 to 90 days as domain authority compounds.

Do you manage ad spend directly or do we pay the ad platforms?

You maintain full ownership of your advertising accounts (Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads). Ad spend is paid directly from your card to the platform, while we charge a transparent monthly campaign management and creative optimization fee.

How do you measure campaign success and return on ad spend (ROAS)?

We set up end-to-end conversion tracking with Google Analytics 4, Meta Pixel, and server-side Conversions API (CAPI). You receive transparent monthly reports detailing cost per lead, conversion rate, and calculated return on investment.
