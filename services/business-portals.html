FAQ

Business Portal Questions Answered

Common questions when commissioning a customized enterprise portal.

Can multiple user roles (Admin, Accountant, Staff, Agent) have different permissions?

Yes. Every portal we build includes granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). You can define exactly which screens, financial figures, customer records, and export buttons each team member can view or edit.

Can this replace our existing manual Excel sheets without losing old records?

Yes! Our engineering team handles complete data migration. We clean, normalize, and import your existing legacy Excel or CSV records into your new secure database before deployment.

Is the portal accessible from mobile phones and tablets?

Yes. All portal interfaces are 100% responsive, optimized for desktop monitors, iPads, and Android/iPhone mobile screens with biometric login options.
