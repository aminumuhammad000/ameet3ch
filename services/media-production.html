FAQ

Media Production Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about booking our video and drone crew.

Can your crew travel for shoots outside Kano?

Yes. Our media crew travels nationwide across Nigeria for corporate shoots, real estate portfolio recordings, documentaries, and executive event coverage.

What equipment does your production team shoot on?

We shoot on professional 4K cinema cameras, DJI 4K drone gimbals, wireless lavalier audio systems, and studio-grade softbox lighting rigs.

What is the turnaround time for the edited final video?

Standard turnaround is 3 to 5 business days after shoot completion. Expedited 24-hour turnaround is also available for press releases and live event teasers.
