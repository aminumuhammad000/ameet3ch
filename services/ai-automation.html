FAQ

AI & Automation Questions Answered

Clear facts on how we integrate AI into existing business workflows.

Can the AI chatbot provide inaccurate information (hallucinations)?

We use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with strict boundary prompting and semantic grounding. The AI only answers questions using verified data from your approved documentation, and gracefully escalates when information is outside its knowledge base.

Can this integrate with our current CRM and WhatsApp numbers?

Yes! We integrate directly with your official WhatsApp Business number via Meta's Cloud API, connecting conversations seamlessly to HubSpot, Google Sheets, Zoho, or your internal MySQL/Postgres databases.

Is our company and customer data kept secure and confidential?

Absolutely. We build with strict data segregation, zero-training enterprise API agreements (ensuring your business data is never used to train public LLM models), and end-to-end TLS encryption.
