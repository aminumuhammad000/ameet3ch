Connecta Talent: AI-Powered Freelance Marketplace & Escrow Contract Platform

A pan-African talent and freelance marketplace linking elite software developers, designers, and visual creators with worldwide clients using AI semantic matching and milestone escrow.

The Challenge & Objective

African tech and creative talent faces significant friction landing international contracts due to payment barriers and credibility verification. Amee Technology built Connecta as an end-to-end marketplace with AI project matching and escrow protection.

Key Features Built by Amee Technology

Every layer of the platform was crafted to combine strict security, intuitive user journeys, and zero technical debt.

  • AI Semantic Skill Matching

    Vector embeddings analyze client job briefs and recommend the top 3 best-suited freelancers instantly.

  • Milestone-Based Escrow Contracts

    Clients fund deliverables in advance; funds release only after work inspection and client sign-off.

  • Automated Portfolio Verification

    Automated GitHub, Dribbble, and Behance sync verifying that submitted code and designs are authentic.

  • Multi-Currency Global Invoicing

    Enables clients in the US, UK, and Europe to pay via Stripe while freelancers receive funds in Naira or USD.

  • Built-In Dispute Mitigation

    Clear revision caps and escrow arbitration protocols that prevent scope creep and non-payment.

  • Direct Work Messaging & File Sharing

    High-speed collaboration workspace with shared file previews and revision version control.
