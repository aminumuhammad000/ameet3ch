Connecta: AI-Powered Local Services & Artisan Marketplace in Kano

Connecting households, tenants, and businesses in Kano and across Nigeria with vetted local service professionals — plumbers, electricians, AC technicians, carpenters, and painters — backed by government ID verification, honest reviews, and milestone escrow security.

The Challenge & Objective

Finding a reliable artisan or technical repair specialist in Kano and across Nigeria traditionally relies on uncertain word-of-mouth referrals, non-transparent pricing, and risky upfront cash payments with zero recourse. Amee Technology re-engineered Connecta as an end-to-end, high-trust local services marketplace connecting households with verified plumbers, electricians, AC technicians, carpenters, and repair specialists through sub-second AI matching and guaranteed milestone escrow.

Key Features Built by Amee Technology

Every layer of the platform was crafted to combine strict identity verification, intuitive user journeys, sub-second AI matchmaking, and secure local escrow payouts.

  • Sub-Second AI Trade Matching (<1s Match Time)

    Homeowners describe repairs in plain words; the matching engine analyzes trade skills, customer ratings, proximity across Kano municipal districts, and budget to surface fit-ranked recommendations instantly.

  • Milestone-Based Escrow Vaults

    Clients fund job milestones up front in escrow; service providers begin work knowing funds are locked and guaranteed. Payouts are released only upon customer inspection and milestone sign-off.

  • Government ID & NIN Verification Badges

    Multi-step identity vetting verifies government credentials, artisan certifications, and workshop locations, awarding official trust badges to safeguard homeowners from fraud.

  • Coordinated Trade Squads ("Collabo Squads")

    For complex residential renovations or solar installations, customers can hire multidisciplinary squads (e.g., master electricians, plumbers, and carpenters) under one unified brief with automatic split payouts.

  • Two-Way Verified Ratings & Human Mediation

    Escrow-tied review cycles ensure genuine ratings feed directly into search rankings. Built-in dispute arbitration locks funds fairly until human evidence evaluation resolves any scope disputes.

  • Instant Local Bank & Mobile Transfers

    Zero hidden platform subscriptions; verified artisans and contractors receive instant, frictionless payouts directly to local Nigerian bank accounts upon job completion.
