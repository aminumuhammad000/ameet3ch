The Challenge & Objective

Sulhab Creative Limited (RC - 10004328) needed a world-class, human-centered digital presence that articulates its core mission: transforming latent potential into real-world vocational capabilities, career opportunities, and economic independence. The platform required clear presentation of their four strategic pillars, seamless student and partner inquiry funnels, and high-performance mobile responsiveness.

Key Features Built by Amee Technology

Engineered for visual elegance, accessibility, and high conversion, every section guides users toward meaningful engagement.

  • Dynamic Full-Screen Hero & Brand System

    Engaging visual hierarchy featuring custom ambient doodles, clean typography, and distinctive glassmorphic badges highlighting student achievements.

  • Four Strategic Pillar Showcase

    Structured modular architecture showcasing Learning & Development, Career Development, Women's Enterprise & Economic Empowerment, and Community Development & Social Impact.

  • Smart Dual Header Navigation

    Full-width hero navigation transitioning into an elegant sticky header on scroll with accessible dropdown menus for deep-dive service pages.

  • Integrated Contact & Onboarding Funnels

    Frictionless communication pathways including automated Web3Forms inquiry handling with bot protection alongside direct one-tap WhatsApp chat onboarding.

  • Performance & Cloud Deployment

    Built with modern semantic standards, lightweight CSS architecture, and deployed via continuous integration on Vercel for instantaneous global load times.

  • Institutional Trust & Corporate Governance

    Prominent display of corporate credentials, mission/vision clarity, and social channel integration (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook).
