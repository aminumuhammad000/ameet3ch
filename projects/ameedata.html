AmeeData was envisioned as the cleanest, most intuitive mobile billing application in Nigeria. Amee Technology designed a frictionless interface that eliminated the tedious multi-step recharge processes common in traditional apps.

Key Features Built by Amee Technology

Every layer of the platform was crafted to combine strict security, intuitive user journeys, and zero technical debt.

  • 2-Tap Recharge Flow

    Smart number detection that automatically identifies telecom network and presets favorite data bundles.

  • Automated Virtual Bank Accounts

    Unique dedicated accounts with instantaneous bank transfer detection and auto-credit.

  • Customer Loyalty & Cashback

    Automated micro-cashback engine rewarding users on every airtime and data transaction.

  • Dark / Light Adaptive Interface

    High-contrast visual design system tested under direct sunlight for outdoor mobile users.

  • Instant Transaction PDF Slips

    One-click generation of verified branded payment receipts for business tax records.

  • Zero-Loss Reversal Engine

    Automated instant refund trigger whenever upstream provider networks fail to deliver.
