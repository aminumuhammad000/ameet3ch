Peeritrade Escrow: Fraud-Proof Peer-to-Peer Escrow Trading Platform

A secure peer-to-peer digital asset and football ticket trading platform with multi-signature escrow holding, encrypted chat, dispute resolution workflows, and zero fraud track record.

The Challenge & Objective

Peer-to-peer commerce in emerging markets is heavily plagued by fraud, payment reversals, and trust deficits. Amee Technology designed and built Peeritrade with a military-grade escrow mechanism that holds seller assets and buyer funds until mutual condition verification.

Key Features Built by Amee Technology

Every layer of the platform was crafted to combine strict security, intuitive user journeys, and zero technical debt.

  • Multi-Step Escrow Lockup

    Assets are cryptographically locked in escrow before the buyer is permitted to transmit payment.

  • Encrypted Real-Time Trade Chat

    End-to-end WebSocket chat with image upload for payment receipts and automated timer warnings.

  • Arbitration & Dispute Portal

    Dedicated admin arbitration desk where certified moderators inspect payment proofs and enforce settlements.

  • Reputation & Trust Badging

    Automated trader rating system based on completed trades, response velocity, and verification levels.

  • Anti-Phishing & Session Shields

    Strict IP location binding, biometric confirmations, and device fingerprinting to prevent account takeovers.

  • Instant Bank Payout Rail

    Automated settlement to local bank accounts once buyer and seller confirm satisfaction.
