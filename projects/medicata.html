The Challenge & Objective

Access to immediate clinical consultation is critically constrained in Africa. Amee Technology engineered Medicata to bridge the healthcare gap using medical NLP that evaluates patient symptoms, assesses emergency severity, and prepares clinical charts for physicians.

Key Features Built by Amee Technology

Every layer of the platform was crafted to combine strict security, intuitive user journeys, and zero technical debt.

  • Conversational Symptom Triage

    Patients describe ailments in plain English or local vernacular; the AI asks clarifying medical questions.

  • Emergency Urgency Classifier

    Red-flags critical symptoms (stroke, cardiac distress, hemorrhage) for immediate emergency intervention.

  • Automated Clinical Notes (SOAP)

    Generates structured Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan summaries for certified doctors.

  • Live Telemedicine Consultations

    Encrypted WebRTC video calling between patients and certified medical specialists.

  • Digital Pharmacy & e-Prescriptions

    Tamper-proof digital prescription codes that patients can present at registered local pharmacies.

  • Patient Health Vault

    Encrypted medical history archive with chronological vitals logs, lab test uploads, and doctor notes.
