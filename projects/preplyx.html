Preplyx: AI-Driven Exam Preparation & CBT Testing Engine

An intelligent assessment and learning platform engineered to help students across Nigeria excel in national examinations (JAMB UTME, WAEC SSCE, NECO) through rigorous timed simulations, mistake analysis, and personalized AI tutoring.

The Challenge & Objective

Millions of candidates write high-stakes examinations annually in Nigeria, yet the majority lack access to accurate simulation environments, immediate feedback, and targeted weakness diagnostics. Preplyx was architected to bridge this gap: building a rock-solid, cheat-resistant CBT runner with server-side validation, zero question-leakage, real-time autosaving, and deep analytical reports.

Key Features Built by Amee Technology

Designed for resilience under spotty connectivity, academic integrity, and engaging student retention.

  • High-Fidelity CBT Exam Simulation

    Authentic timed exam runner mimicking official JAMB/WAEC interfaces with keyboard navigation, question flagging, and instant countdown timers.

  • Bulletproof Academic Integrity & Anti-Tamper State Machine

    Sanitized client projections that eliminate answer scraping, combined with a strict server-enforced state machine rejecting double-submissions and late answers.

  • Diagnostic Mistake & Trap Engine

    Pinpoints why answers were missed, categorizing common exam traps, concept misconceptions, and timing bottlenecks to accelerate mastery.

  • Intelligent Progress & Percentile Analytics

    Subject-by-subject strength meters, historical score trajectories, and benchmark comparisons against nationwide candidate percentiles.

  • Resilient Session Persistence & Autosave

    Idempotent local-and-remote state synchronization preventing student work loss during sudden network dropouts or battery drain.

  • Responsive High-Speed Frontend

    Engineered with modern React and Tailwind CSS for instant touch response, minimal data consumption, and buttery smooth 60fps animations.
