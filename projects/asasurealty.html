ASASU Realty Portal: Real Estate Sales Tracking & Automated Agent Commission Suite

A dedicated enterprise property sales, agent hierarchy, and multi-tier commission calculation portal built for ASASU Realty to eliminate spreadsheet disputes and automate agent payouts.

The Challenge & Objective

ASASU Realty manages an expansive network of real estate agents and high-value property portfolios. Manual calculations caused delays and friction. Amee Technology engineered a custom portal that calculates tiered commissions instantly upon property sale verification.

Key Features Built by Amee Technology

Every layer of the platform was crafted to combine strict security, intuitive user journeys, and zero technical debt.

  • Multi-Level Agent Hierarchy

    Supports primary sales agents, team leaders, and regional agency overrides with automated splits.

  • Property Sales Ledger

    Centralized database of all estate plots, payment milestones, deed transfers, and buyer documentation.

  • Instant Commission Calculator

    Eliminates calculation errors by applying contract percentage formulas automatically on deposit receipt.

  • Agent Mobile Dashboard

    Realtors track sales progress, pending disbursements, and download official payment certificates from their phones.

  • One-Click Bank Payouts

    Direct integration with corporate banking APIs to disburse approved agent commissions in a single click.

  • Tamper-Proof Audit Logging

    Every property price change, contract approval, and payment voucher is permanently logged.
