The Challenge & Objective

Northern Creative Designers Summit needed a world-class digital home to reflect the renaissance of Arewa creative design, celebrate local design pioneers, and facilitate ticket bookings, keynote schedules, hackathon sprints, and creative design awards in Kano, Nigeria.

Key Features Built by Amee Technology

Every layer of the platform was crafted to combine strict security, intuitive user journeys, and zero technical debt.

  • Arewa Motif Digital Identity

    Bespoke digital design system incorporating traditional northern Nigerian motifs into modern CSS and vector graphics.

  • Tailored Attendee Pathways

    Dynamic interactive filtering for founders, UI/UX designers, developers, and agency executives.

  • Live Hackathon & Sprint Schedules

    Detailed agenda breakdowns, speaker profiles, and live stage session countdown timers.
