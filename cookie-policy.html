1. What Are Cookies and Local Browser Storage?

HTTP Cookies are tiny text files placed on your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you browse websites. Cookies help the website recognize your device, maintain security, remember your settings, and evaluate how visitors interact with different pages.

Browser Local Storage (HTML5 LocalStorage): In addition to traditional cookies, modern web applications utilize client-side browser storage. This allows us to temporarily preserve drafts (such as invoices or business diagnostics) entirely on your device without transmitting data over the internet to remote databases.

2. Why We Use Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Amee Technology Ltd uses cookies and local storage to:

  • Ensure the technical stability, speed, and cybersecurity of our web platforms;
  • Protect against automated spam submissions, CSRF attacks, and malicious requests;
  • Store your privacy preferences and cookie choices so you are not prompted repeatedly;
  • Allow you to create and edit business documents in Ameetech Toolkits offline without server retention;
  • Collect aggregated, anonymous telemetry to understand which technical guides and business tools deliver the greatest value to Nigerian MSMEs and global visitors.

3. Categories of Cookies We Use

A. Strictly Necessary Cookies (Essential)

These cookies and storage elements are vital for the proper operation and cybersecurity of our website. They enable core functions such as session identification, security routing, and remembering whether you have accepted or rejected optional cookies. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) and GDPR, strictly necessary cookies do not require prior consent because they are essential to provide the service requested by the user.

B. Performance & Analytics Cookies (Optional)

These cookies help us measure visitor traffic, understand referral sources, identify broken pages, and optimize page load speeds. We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4). All IP addresses are automatically pseudonymized, and analytics metrics are aggregated without linking to personal contact information. These cookies are disabled by default and only activated if you click "Accept All" or toggle Analytics in preferences.

C. Functional & Client Storage Elements (Optional)

These elements enhance usability and user experience by storing temporary states (e.g., AmeeBot assessment answers or unfinished invoice fields in Toolkits) in your browser's local memory so that refreshing the page does not erase your work.

5. Google Analytics & Consent Mode v2

We deploy Google Analytics 4 in full compliance with Google Consent Mode v2:

  • When you first land on our website, tracking tags are instructed to treat analytics_storage, ad_storage, and ad_personalization as denied.
  • No analytical cookies are read or set until you grant permission.
  • If you click "Reject Non-Essential", our script actively deletes existing _ga and _ga_* cookies from your browser session and maintains the denied status.
  • If you click "Accept All", our consent manager updates Google Consent Mode to granted for analytical telemetry.

6. Ameetech Toolkits Client Storage Privacy

Our standalone productivity platform at toolkits.ameetechnology.com.ng is engineered with a strict zero remote database model:

  • No accounts or passwords are required to generate invoices, receipts, quotations, or WhatsApp links.
  • Your customer details, bank account numbers, itemized line items, and pricing are computed in real time in your browser memory.
  • Documents are rendered directly to PDF via client-side JavaScript.
  • No copies of generated PDFs or financial transactions are ever sent to or archived on Amee Technology servers.

7. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You have multiple ways to control cookies on our website:

A. Use Our Cookie Preferences Manager

Click the button below to review or update your consent settings at any time:

B. Adjust Your Web Browser Settings

You can configure your browser to block cookies, notify you before a cookie is set, or delete all existing cookies. Step-by-step instructions for major browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-party cookies > Block third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection > Strict.
  • Apple Safari (macOS / iOS): Settings > Safari > Privacy & Security > Block All Cookies / Prevent Cross-Site Tracking.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies and site data.
  • Brave Browser: Brave Shields automatically blocks non-essential cross-site trackers by default.

C. Opt-Out of Google Analytics Universally

You can permanently opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
